# Store listing (draft)

**Name:** Groovelight

**Short description (80 characters):** Your Mac's music library on your Android player or phone, synced over Wi-Fi.

**Full description:**

Groovelight brings the music you choose in Groovelight on your Mac to your Android music player or phone, over your
own Wi-Fi. No account, no cloud, no subscription.

- Play it with Groovelight: albums, playlists and the sound you know from the Mac, and plays go back to the Mac.
- Or keep your favorite player app: Groovelight only syncs, and FiiO Music, HiBy Music, Poweramp or any other player plays it.
- Hi-res FLAC at full resolution, gapless albums, the songs on internal storage or the SD card.
- Only what changed is copied, automatically on Wi-Fi if you like.

Requires Groovelight for macOS (free and open source: https://github.com/irwingbraga/groovelight) on a Mac on the same
network.

**Category:** Music & Audio. **Contains ads:** no. **In-app purchases:** no.

**Content rating questionnaire:** a utility for the user's own music; no user-generated content shared between users,
no violence, no gambling. **Target audience:** 13 and older (not designed for children).

## Review notes

Groovelight needs Groovelight on a Mac to sync music. For review without a Mac, the app offers a demo library
(planned with the player: a few Creative Commons albums) that plays without pairing. Foreground services:
`mediaPlayback` (playing music, with the media notification) and `dataSync` (syncing with the Mac, with a progress
notification); videos of both go with the declaration.
