# Groovelight for Android: privacy policy

Draft, 2026-10-18. Applies to the Groovelight app for Android (application id `dev.irwingbraga.groovelight.sync`).

**Groovelight collects no personal data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
Nothing is sent to its developer or to any third party.

## What the app does with data

- **Your Mac.** The app talks only to Groovelight on your own Mac, over your local network, after you pair them by
  comparing a code on both screens. It sends the Mac this device's name and model, its storage space, the list of
  files in the music folder you chose, and (in a later version) what you played, so the Mac can update play counts.
  It receives music files and information about them from the Mac.
- **On this device.** The app keeps the key shared with your Mac, the Mac's name and address, the folder you chose,
  your settings, the time of the last sync, and a database of the music on the device. None of it is included in
  Android backups or moved to a new device.
- **Your music folder.** The app reads and writes only the folder you picked, and removes only files it put there.
- **Last.fm.** When you use Last.fm with Groovelight, plays from this device are sent to Last.fm by your Mac, under your
  Mac's settings, not by this app.

## Permissions

Network access (to reach your Mac on the local network), notifications (sync progress and playback controls),
foreground services (syncing and playing with the screen off) and starting after a restart (automatic syncs).
