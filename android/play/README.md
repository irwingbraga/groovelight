# Google Play paperwork (drafts)

Groovelight for Android is not on Google Play. These drafts keep it ready to submit: update them whenever the app
starts doing something they do not describe (a new permission, data leaving the device, a new service).

- [`privacy-policy.md`](privacy-policy.md): to publish (GitHub Pages) and link from the store listing.
- [`data-safety.md`](data-safety.md): answers for the Play Console's Data safety form.
- [`listing.md`](listing.md): store listing text, content rating and review notes.

Checked by CI on every release: `checkPlayReleasePolicy` (permissions and target API) and `lintPlayRelease`.
