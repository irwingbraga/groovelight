## Highlights

- **Jump to a letter.** Artists and Songs in Groovelight for Android have an A to Z index along the right edge: tap or drag along it to reach a letter at once.

## Groovelight for Android

- **A to Z index:** beside Artists and Songs (the phone's and the Mac's library) once they have 40 or more entries. A tap or a drag along it jumps to that letter (or the next one that has entries), with a tick at each new letter and the letter shown large beside the finger; letters with nothing under them are dimmed. In short windows it shows every second or third letter, and a drag still reaches them all. Songs follow their sort order (title, artist or album); sorted by date added, they keep the fast scroller.
- **Letters without accents:** names starting with an accented letter are filed under the plain letter ("Élodie" under E), and those starting with a digit or a symbol under #.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.6.2.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.6.2.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
