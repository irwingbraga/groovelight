## Highlights

- **Groovelight for Android is a player now.** The Android app, until now Groovelight Sync, plays your music itself, with the library as your Mac knows it: albums, artists, playlists, credits, synced lyrics, loves and ratings, the 15-band equalizer, gapless playback and crossfade. Install `Groovelight-0.5.0.apk` (attached below) over Groovelight Sync: pairing and music stay, and you choose Player or Sync only.
- **Your whole Mac library on your phone.** Browse everything on the Mac from the phone and add albums, artists or songs to it, even while the Mac is away (they go when it is back).
- **Smaller copies for phones, players and cards.** Any device can get lossless songs as Opus 160, AAC 192 or MP3 320 kbps, album by album.
- **The phone talks back.** Plays (scrobbled to Last.fm with the time they happened), skips, loves, ratings and playlist edits made on the phone reach the Mac's library.

## Groovelight for Android

- **Library:** albums, artists, songs and playlists from the Mac, with their covers, the artists' photos and the credits ("Produced by", and a page for each person). Search covers titles, artists and everyone credited.
- **One library:** the Mac's music and the phone's together (with Downloaded off): what is on the phone has a check, the rest can be added from its page, in its original format or a lossy one.
- **Now Playing:** on the cover's colors, with synced lyrics, Up Next (reorder, remove), love and rating, speed, a sleep timer, the audio path (the file's format and what reaches Android), and visualizers (Bars, Waves, and on Android 13 and later Lights and Tunnel). It grows out of the mini player and follows your finger.
- **Sound:** the 15-band parametric equalizer with the Mac's presets and AutoEq files, Sound Check by song or album, gapless albums, crossfade between other songs (Settings, Sound), and bit-perfect output to USB DACs on Android 14 and later.
- **Playlists:** make, fill, rename and delete them on the phone (the Mac's library follows); smart playlists update on the phone as you play, love and rate.
- **The Shelf:** Cover Flow, as on the Mac, always dark.
- **Around the phone:** home screen widgets (Now Playing, Recently Played), a Quick Settings tile, Android Auto, and voice search ("play ... on Groovelight").
- **Look:** Material 3 in Groovelight's colors, frosted glass bars, light, dark or the system's theme, layouts for landscape and split screen (half or a third of the screen beside a map in a car).
- **Updates itself:** the app checks GitHub's releases once a day and offers new versions (Settings, App Updates), checked before installing.

## Sync

- **Encrypted:** wireless sync now runs over HTTPS, with a certificate the phone pins when pairing; every file is checked after it arrives.
- **Formats every phone plays:** for the player, ALAC, AIFF, WavPack and other formats Android cannot play are converted to FLAC as they sync (24-bit kept), and CUE albums are split into one song per track.
- **Lossy copies:** Opus 160, AAC 192 or MP3 320 kbps, chosen on the device's page on the Mac (also for memory cards and DAPs) or on the phone, for the whole device or album by album. Songs that are already lossy are copied as they are.
- **Remove from the phone:** removing a song, album, artist or playlist on the phone keeps it off that phone (the Mac's library keeps everything); the device's page shows those songs and can bring them back.
- **Fixed:** "unexpected end of stream" while syncing (the Mac now waits for the phone to read each answer in full), and covers some phones could not show are sent in a form they can.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.5.0.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over Groovelight Sync 0.4 and earlier, keeping its pairing and music. It needs Groovelight 0.5.0 on the Mac for the player (Sync only works with earlier versions too). The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.5.0.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
