## Library

- **Albums as a list on your phone:** on Android and iOS, the Albums tab can now show a compact list, a row per album with its cover, title, artist and year, so many more fit on the screen. Choose **View as: List** in the sort menu on Android, or in the new **View As** menu on iOS; the grid stays one tap away, and each device remembers your choice.

## Mac

- **Dock menu:** right-click Groovelight in the Dock, as with Music, to see the song playing, love it, play or pause, skip, and turn Shuffle and Repeat on or off.

## Stream Deck and remotes

- **Loved Songs on a key:** the [OpenDeck plugin](https://github.com/irwingbraga/opendeck-groovelight)'s Play key now lists Loved Songs first among the playlists. Playing the playlist "Loved Songs" works from the command line (`groovelight play --playlist "Loved Songs"`) and from Claude too, on the Mac and Windows.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.8-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.8.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.8.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
- **OpenDeck:** see the [plugin's page](https://github.com/irwingbraga/opendeck-groovelight#install).
