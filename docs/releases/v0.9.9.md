## Backups

- **Backups on a NAS work again:** on the Mac, backups to a NAS could stop at the covers with "you don't have permission to access Artwork", when the share refused the extra details macOS keeps with some files. Covers are now copied the way the music is, contents only, so the next backup goes through.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.9-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.9.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.9.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
- **OpenDeck:** see the [plugin's page](https://github.com/irwingbraga/opendeck-groovelight#install).
