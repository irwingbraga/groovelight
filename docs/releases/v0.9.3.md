## Android, iPhone and iPad

- **Every album you ask for arrives:** asking for several albums, artists or songs from the Mac's or the PC's library one after another no longer stops after the first. A request made while a sync runs now brings one more sync when it ends, so the rest download without syncing by hand. On Android, the automatic sync and the Sync button also no longer run at the same time.
- **Play songs as they arrive:** a sync now brings the library first, then adds each song (with its album's cover) every few seconds as it downloads, so you can play an album while the rest of the sync goes on. Songs removed from the device leave the library at the start of the sync.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.3-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.3.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.3.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
