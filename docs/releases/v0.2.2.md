## Highlights

- **Runs on macOS 15 (Sequoia).** Groovelight now supports macOS 15 and later. On macOS 26 it keeps Liquid Glass; on macOS 15 it uses the classic translucent look.
- **Your library is safer on external drives.** A drive that has trouble reading no longer makes songs disappear from the library.
- **Fix Metadata understands mixed-up albums.** Songs of one album imported with different album tags are found and fixed together as one album.
- **Compilations in Artists**, a much faster Songs list, and clickable artist and album names in the player bar.

## Library and external drives

- Songs are only removed from the library when their files are really gone. Before, a drive returning read errors (a failing disk, a loose cable) could hide folders from a check, and their songs were removed with their play counts, loves and playlist entries.
- If a drive cannot be read, the background loudness measuring (for Sound Check style leveling) now pauses after a few failures instead of opening every song on the drive.
- Settings, Library: each music folder now has its own remove button and a right-click menu (Show in Finder, Remove from Library). Removing a folder asks first and says how many songs leave the library. Each folder also shows its song count. Before, folders could not be selected, so they could not be removed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If songs from an external drive disappeared with an earlier version, restore them with their history from a backup made before it happened: Settings, Backup, Restore (Groovelight makes one every week). Check that the drive reads fine first (Disk Utility, First Aid).

## Fix Metadata

- When the selected songs name different albums, Groovelight also identifies a few of them one by one, so the release that holds all of them comes first. The sheet says when songs from several albums are being fixed as one.
- Songs that are not on the chosen release can still take the album's details (album, album artist, year, genre), keeping their own title and number.
- Clearing the Album or Artist box now searches without it, instead of falling back to the songs' tags.
- A progress bar shows how many songs have been written while the changes are applied.

## Browsing

- **Compilations** appear at the top of the Artists list when the library has compilation albums, with their own icon and page. Compilation songs show their own artist, and "Various Artists" links open Compilations.
- **Songs list:** sorting by any column is now instant (sorting 1,000 songs by title took over 20 seconds), and fast scrolling is smooth.
- **Player bar:** the artist and album under the song title link to their pages. On Artists and Genres the bar is centered across the list and the page.

## macOS 15

- Liquid Glass elements fall back to blur materials and standard buttons.
- The equalizer's band sliders, the "⋯" buttons and the player bar on an empty library were fixed for macOS 15.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). The build is not notarized yet: the first time, macOS blocks it; open System Settings, Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". Groovelight 0.2.1 updates itself to this version.

The Android companion app, Groovelight Sync, is attached as an APK (Android 12 or later).
