## Highlights

- **Play On.** From Groovelight for Android, play on the Mac or on another phone with Groovelight open: start and pause, skip, seek and set the volume there, and move the music between them, at the same place. While the Mac plays, any song of its library plays there, whether it is on the phone or not.
- **Away from home.** Phones you allow reach the Mac through Tailscale, a private network between your devices: they sync and play on the Mac as they do at home. Pairing still happens only at home.
- **Drag to sync.** Drag an album or songs onto a device in the sidebar: they join what it holds, and it syncs.

## Mac

- **Drag and drop to devices:** album covers (Albums, search, artist and album pages) and songs (several at once when selected) dropped on a device in the sidebar are added to its selection, as if chosen on its page, and it syncs (a phone when its app next checks in). Songs removed from that device come back.
- **Playlists:** a whole album can now be dropped on a playlist or on Loved Songs, and dragging selected songs takes all of them.
- **Device pages:** "Let ... play music on this Mac and your other devices" (Play On) and "Let ... connect away from home" (Tailscale), both off until turned on, for each phone.
- **Away from home:** requests from outside the Mac's own networks are refused unless the device is allowed, and pairing is refused there. The Mac gives its Tailscale addresses to its devices at each connection.

## Groovelight for Android

- **Play On:** choose the Mac or another phone under Play On. Now Playing and the mini player follow the music there; songs of the Mac's library that are not on the phone play on the Mac with the rest of their album, and its album pages offer Play and Shuffle on the Mac.
- **Other phones can play here:** with Groovelight open, the phone can be chosen from your other devices ("Let other devices play here", on the Sync page).
- **The Mac's library by default:** after pairing, the Library shows the Mac's whole library; the download symbol in the toolbar switches to what is on the phone.
- **Away from home:** the app tries the Mac's Tailscale address when the Mac is not at home or on the network. Settings shows when it is connected away, and "Sync over Mobile Data" (off: syncing away waits for Wi-Fi).
- **Fixed:** the mini player no longer puts itself away while the music plays on the Mac, and songs playing there that are not on the phone show their cover.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.6.0.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. Play On and access away from home need Groovelight 0.6.0 on the Mac. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.6.0.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
