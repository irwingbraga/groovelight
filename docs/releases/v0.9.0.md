## Groovelight for Windows

Groovelight now runs on Windows 11 (x64 and ARM64), with the same library, playback and looks as the Mac, in a Windows 11 (Fluent) interface.

- **The whole player:** Home, Recently Added, Artists, Albums, Songs, Genres, the Shelf, playlists and smart playlists, search with credits, Get Info, Fix Metadata from MusicBrainz, organizing files with undo, import of songs, folders and archives, backups.
- **Sound:** gapless playback and crossfade, bit-perfect output through WASAPI (exclusive mode on request), the 15-band parametric EQ with AutoEq import, Sound Check, speed and the sleep timer. FFmpeg decodes every format the Mac plays.
- **Now Playing:** the cover, the turntable, the cassette deck (your own cassettes too), the CD player and the PC Player, synced lyrics, Up Next, the visualizers and the mini player.
- **Devices and phones:** sync to DAPs and SD cards, and Groovelight for Android pairs with the PC as it does with the Mac (sync, your whole library on the phone, Play On).
- **Last.fm, the `groovelight` command and the MCP server** for Claude, as on the Mac.
- **Updates itself** from GitHub releases when installed.

## Home

A new Home on the Mac, Windows and Android (and in Groovelight for iPhone and iPad, in development), worked out on your computer from your own listening, with no account and no server.

- **Song, Album and Artist of the Day:** songs you used to love and have not heard in a while, loved songs you hardly play, new additions, albums released a round number of years ago, and the artists you play most, each with the reason it was picked ("8 plays, last heard 2 months ago"). A pick stays all day; Something Else shows a few more. An artist's pick plays their essentials.
- **Your Listening:** for the last week, month, year or all time, listening time by day split by where you listened (this computer, phones, Last.fm), when you listen (weekday and hour), plays by place, top songs, albums and artists, genres, decades, your streak of days and the songs new to you.
- **A listening log:** every play and skip is now kept with its time, on the computer and from the phones. Play counts from before are kept as they were.
- **Last.fm history:** Settings, Last.fm, History imports your scrobbles in the background (about 9 minutes for 100,000), matching them to your songs even when names differ a little ("Remastered", "feat.", accents). Plays Groovelight sent to Last.fm are not counted twice, and play counts in your library do not change. New scrobbles are fetched every 6 hours.
- **On the phone:** Home is the first tab on Android (and on iPhone, first in the iPad's sidebar), from the Mac or the PC, kept for when it is away. Play the picks there or on the computer, or bring them to the phone.
- The Mac and the PC pick exactly the same songs for the same library, and Home opens with Shift-Command-H (Ctrl+Shift+H on Windows).

## Claude and the command line

- `groovelight today` shows the picks of the day and why; `groovelight stats --range week|month|year|all` summarizes your listening.
- Two new MCP tools, `todays_picks` and `listening_stats`: ask Claude "what's my album of the day? play it" or "what did I listen to most this year?".

## Fixes

- Artist pages missing their Wikipedia story and photo: when Wikipedia asked Groovelight to slow down, the artist was saved as having no article and not looked up again for 30 days. Requests are now paced and retried, only a real "no article" is kept, and artists saved that way are looked up again.
- Groovelight now identifies itself to MusicBrainz and Wikipedia with its project page when no contact is set in Settings, Metadata, as both services ask.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.0-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing: one file with everything in it, which does not update itself and has no `groovelight` command.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.0.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.0.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
