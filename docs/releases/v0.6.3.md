## Groovelight for Android

- **Fixed:** an artist's photo at the top of their page (and a person's, from the credits) had a square shadow behind the round photo; the shadow is round now.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.6.3.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.6.3.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
