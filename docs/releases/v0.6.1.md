## Highlights

- **Groovelight for Android on foldables and tablets.** Unfolded, or on a tablet, the app gets a sidebar like the iPad's, Now Playing with the lyrics or Up Next beside the cover, and a player bar along the bottom. Folded, Now Playing fits the short cover screen.
- **Lyrics while the Mac plays.** A song playing on the Mac that is not on the phone now shows its lyrics in Now Playing, as well as its cover.

## Mac

- **Now Playing:** Shuffle and Repeat beside the transport, as in the player bar, lit while on.
- **Devices:** a phone showing a song it does not have (playing on the Mac) gets its lyrics from the Mac, which looks them up online first when the library has none, and whether it is loved.

## Groovelight for Android

- **Sidebar (from 840 dp wide):** an unfolded foldable held wide or a tablet has the iPad's sidebar in place of the tabs: Search, the Library's sections (Albums, Artists, Songs, Playlists), the Shelf, Loved Songs and each playlist, and Settings.
- **Player bar (from 600 dp wide):** in place of the mini player, a bar docked along the bottom of the page: the song with its cover and quality, the progress along its top edge, shuffle, previous, play, next and repeat, and Lyrics, Play On and Up Next, which open Now Playing on that panel. Controls drop out as the window narrows.
- **Now Playing:** in wide windows, the cover and the controls with the lyrics or Up Next beside them. On a tall unfolded screen it keeps the phone's layout in a centered column. On a short screen such as a passport-shaped foldable's cover screen, the cover now fits instead of covering the title.
- **Lyrics from the Mac:** a song of the Mac's library that is not on the phone shows its lyrics in Now Playing while it plays on the Mac.
- **Fixed:** with a larger text or display size, the Shelf's scrubber and the bottom of lists no longer go under the mini player, and the player bar grows with the text.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.6.1.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. Lyrics of the Mac's songs not on the phone need Groovelight 0.6.1 on the Mac. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.6.1.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
