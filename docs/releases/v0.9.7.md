## Stream Deck

- **Groovelight on your deck:** the new [Groovelight plugin for OpenDeck](https://github.com/irwingbraga/opendeck-groovelight), the open source Stream Deck software, gives you Play / Pause, Next and Previous keys, a Now Playing key with the cover, title and artist, keys that play a playlist, an album, an artist or a search (shuffled if you like), and an Album of the Day key. They follow the app within a second, however you play, and checking them never starts Groovelight.
- **Covers for remotes:** `groovelight artwork <song-id>` saves a song's cover (or prints it in base64 with `--json`), and `now-playing` tells which cover is showing, so the keys fetch each cover only once. Older versions of the app work with the plugin without covers.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.7-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.7.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.7.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
- **OpenDeck:** see the [plugin's page](https://github.com/irwingbraga/opendeck-groovelight#install).
