## Android, iPhone and iPad

- **Album pages as an open sleeve:** on a foldable held open (a Galaxy Z Fold, the iPhone Duo's inner display) or a tablet, an album's page now has two sides, as an open LP sleeve: the cover as large as its side allows, the title, artist, details, Play and Shuffle staying put on one, and the songs (by disc) and credits scrolling on the other. On the iPhone Duo each side stays on its side of the fold.
- **Album pages over their cover:** on phones and folded, the cover is larger, over its own colors (the cover itself, very large and blurred, fading into the page). On iPhone, the songs are now grouped by disc, with the count and length at the end, as on Android.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.5-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.5.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.5.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
