## Groovelight for Android

- **Now Playing:** "Artist — Album" under the song's title, as on the iPhone, each opening its page: the phone's when the phone has it, the Mac's otherwise. Too long for one line, one under the other. Go to Album and Go to Artist in the menu open the same pages.
- **Swipe to change songs:** swipe the cover left for the next song, right for the previous one (or the start of this one, as the Previous button does). A short swipe springs back.

## Groovelight for iPhone and iPad

- **Swipe to change songs:** in Now Playing, swipe the cover left for the next song, right for the previous one. Pulling down still closes Now Playing.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.6.4.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.6.4.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
