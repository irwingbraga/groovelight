## Classical music by composer

- **Composers:** classical albums are now listed by composer, in a Composers section beside Artists, instead of under a different performer on every record (a soloist, an orchestra, a conductor). One composer's albums are together however they were recorded, each with who plays it, and a recital with several composers is under each of them with their pieces.
- **Found from the tags:** an album is classical when its genre says so (Classical, Clássica, Baroque, Opera, Música de concerto...) and it has a composer; "Classic Rock" and "Neoclassical Metal" are not, and a band with one album tagged Classical keeps it under the band. Short names join the full one ("Vivaldi" and "Antonio Vivaldi"). On the Mac, an album's "List In" choice (its "..." menu) decides when the tags do not.
- **On Android, iPhone and iPad:** Composers is there too, as the Mac lists them (with its choices), from the next sync with the Mac.

## Fixing metadata

- **Much faster on external drives:** a new cover is saved once, as the album folder's cover.jpg, instead of into every song (which made each file be rewritten whole, seconds a song on a slow drive). Songs in a folder with others keep it embedded.
- **In the background:** Fix Metadata and Get Info close at once; the sidebar shows the songs being written, and fixes made in a row wait their turn. Quitting while they are saved asks first, and can stop after the song being written.
- **Importing alongside:** an import now checks only the folders its songs went to, not the whole library, and the library's own checks wait while tags are written, so both go faster at the same time.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.6-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.6.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.6.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
