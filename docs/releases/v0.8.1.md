## Now Playing on the Mac

- **Love and Up Next:** the cover's view has a heart and an Up Next button under its controls, as on the iPhone. Up Next opens beside the cover, where the lyrics show, and the Up Next shortcut opens it there while Now Playing is up.
- **On the players too:** the turntable, cassette deck and CD player have a heart cap at the foot of their panel and an Up Next cap at the top, the queue showing in the player's place like the lyrics. The PC Player keeps its own heart key and playlist.

## Android

- **The same icon as the Mac and iOS:** the icon's navy gradient and its glass note, rendered from the Apple icon, in place of the hand-drawn copy. Themed icons get the same note, flat.

## Fixes

- The PC Player's logo builds with the stricter checks of Xcode's editor.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.8.1.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.8.1.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
