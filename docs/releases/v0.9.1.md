## Android

- **Groovelight for Android is back in the release,** with everything from 0.9.0: Home (Song, Album and Artist of the Day and Your Listening, from the Mac or the PC) as the first tab, and pairing with a PC as with a Mac. The 0.9.0 APK did not build.
- **Tapping a song plays from it, in order:** playing a song of another album or playlist no longer keeps the last one's shuffle. The songs after it follow, and shuffle turns off, as in other music apps; Shuffle still plays a list shuffled.

## iPhone, iPad and Android

- **Lyrics keep the screen on:** while Now Playing shows a song's lyrics, the screen no longer dims and locks, so you can read or sing along. Closing the lyrics, or a song without any, lets it sleep again.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; 0.9.0 updates itself. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.1-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.1.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.1.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
