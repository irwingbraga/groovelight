## Mac

- **Artists keeps the player bar in place:** opening an album from an artist no longer moves the floating player bar to the left, over the artist list.
- **Choosing another artist shows them:** with an album open on Artists (or Genres), choosing another artist (or genre) in the list closes the album and shows the one you chose, as in Music. Choosing the same artist again goes back to their page.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.2-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.2.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.2.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
