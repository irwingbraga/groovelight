## Mac: backups to a NAS

Groovelight can now keep a copy of your data and your music on a NAS (or another disk) by itself, at night, so the NAS's drives wake once a night and sleep the rest of the day. [docs/NAS.md](https://github.com/irwingbraga/groovelight/blob/main/docs/NAS.md) shows the setup, with a UGREEN DXP2800 as the example.

- **A NAS folder for automatic backups:** data backups go in its `Data` folder (ones made there before move in), written under a temporary name and renamed when complete, so a backup cut short never counts. The covers are kept once beside the backups and only new ones are copied, so a nightly backup takes seconds. Settings, Backups shows the copy's progress, the folder, its free space, whether it is reachable now and why not, when the next backup runs, and the last failure.
- **Groovelight's own login to the NAS:** a user and password for the shared folder (the password in your Keychain). Groovelight connects with them when the share is not connected, with no dialog, and says clearly when the NAS refuses them.
- **At night:** daily or weekly, between 2:00 and 6:00 by default, or any time; a night missed is made up later in the day; optionally only when the Mac is plugged in.
- **Quitting during a backup:** Groovelight asks whether to quit when it is done, now, or not at all.
- **Also when Groovelight is closed:** Groovelight opens hidden at night when a backup is due, then quits (allowed once in System Settings, Login Items).
- **Your music too:** the library folders you choose are copied to the backup's `Music` folder as they are. Only new and changed files are copied, renamed files are moved, and songs you remove are set aside for 7, 30 or 90 days (or forever) instead of deleted. A folder on an unplugged drive is left alone, and one that lost many files waits for you. New copies are read back and checked, and a sample is checked again every month. The first copy goes on over several nights if it has to.
- **Getting music back:** play a library folder from its copy on the NAS when its drive is lost, copy it back to a new drive, or put back songs removed by mistake (Settings, Backups, Music, Restore…). After restoring on a new Mac, Use NAS Copy plays a folder from the NAS at once.
- **Command line and Claude:** `groovelight backup status` and `groovelight backup now`, and the `backup_status` and `back_up_now` tools.

Backups to a NAS are not in Groovelight for Windows yet.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.4-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.4.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.4.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
