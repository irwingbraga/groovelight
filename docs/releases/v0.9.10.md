## Playback

- **Bit-perfect playback keeps stereo:** on the Mac, with bit-perfect on, switching the output device to a song's sample rate could put it in a mono format when the device offers mono and stereo at the same depth (some USB speakers do). The device now keeps its channels, and one left in mono goes back to stereo with the next song.
- **No more stutters from Sound Check:** on the Mac, measuring the loudness of songs without ReplayGain tags read the whole library from the music drive while music played, and a busy external drive made playback stutter. The songs waiting to be measured now wait for the music to pause; the songs playing and next are measured from memory when they are there.
- **Memory buffer:** a new option in Settings, Playback, off by default, reads the next song whole into memory while one plays, so a busy or slow drive (an external disk during a backup, a network share) cannot interrupt it. Only the songs playing and next are kept; songs over 400 MB still play from the drive.

## Home

- **Artist of the Day stays in its place:** on the Mac, an artist photo that is not square could make its card wider or taller than the others and overlap the Album of the Day. Photos are now cropped to their square.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.
- **Windows:** `Groovelight-win-Setup.exe` (x64) or `Groovelight-win-arm64-Setup.exe` (ARM64) installs Groovelight for your account and keeps it up to date; earlier versions update themselves. Or run `Groovelight-0.9.10-win-x64-Portable.exe` (or the ARM64 one) from anywhere without installing.
- **Android:** `Groovelight-0.9.10.apk` (Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music.
- **Claude Desktop:** `Groovelight-0.9.10.mcpb` installs the MCP server.
- **OpenDeck:** see the [plugin's page](https://github.com/irwingbraga/opendeck-groovelight#install).
