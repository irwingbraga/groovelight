## The PC Player

A new look for Now Playing, on the Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android: a desktop music player from the turn of the millennium, on a wallpaper of a green hill under a blue sky. Choose it, and its theme, with the look button at the top of Now Playing.

- **Classic:** the two-window skin of the late 1990s, drawn after the base skin's own colors and keys. The green display with the time (tap for the time left) and a spectrum, the song's title scrolling by, the bit rate and sample rate, the volume and balance sliders, EQ and PL, the gold position bar, the transport keys, a heart to love the song, and Groovelight's notes as the logo. The playlist window docks under it: your queue in green, the song playing in white, a tap to play another.
- **Corona:** the glassy blue player of 2002. The screen shows the spectrum or the album's cover, the green strip cycles the title, artist and album, and the transport drawer has the large round play, stop, previous and next, mute, the volume wedge and the seek bar. The playlist drawer slides out beside the window on an iPad or a Mac, and under it on a phone.

## The vintage players, more real

- **CD player:** the disc spins up and slows down like a real one, with no more hiccups, and has a real disc's proportions: a 15 mm hole and a thin clear hub.
- **Turntable:** a chrome tonearm, a grooved record that catches the light, smoother turning, and the song in a receiver's amber dial window, its red pointer showing where you are (drag it to go elsewhere).
- **Cassette deck:** molded plastic, paper labels, and gold and silver foil on the premium tapes (pick foil for your own cassettes too). Backlit VU meters with printed scales. Holding rewind or fast forward plays the tape racing past the heads; turn it off in Settings.
- **Volume knobs everywhere:** swipe up or down on a knob to turn it. On the iPhone and iPad the knob now sets the device's own volume.
- **Cleaner screens:** the players no longer show a separate bar under them on the iPhone and iPad (AirPlay, Play On, Up Next and Love moved to the buttons at the top and onto the player), nor the playlist's name at the top on Android.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.8.0.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.8.0.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
