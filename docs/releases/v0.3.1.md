## Fixes

- **Groovelight 0.3.0 could quit right at launch.** On some launches the app tried to apply its light or dark theme before macOS had finished starting it, and stopped. The theme is now applied once the app has launched.
- **Your Last.fm sign-in and paired devices carry over from earlier versions.** Their keys are now read after launch, so macOS can ask to allow access to them. Before, that question was dismissed on its own during startup, and Last.fm looked signed out.

## Updating

- If 0.3.0 quits at launch for you, download the DMG below and drag Groovelight to Applications, replacing 0.3.0. Later versions update themselves again.
- The first time, macOS may ask to allow Groovelight to use its saved keychain items (the Last.fm sign-in, keys of paired Android devices). Click "Always Allow" and you stay signed in.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). It is signed and notarized, so it opens with a normal double-click.

The Android companion app, Groovelight Sync, is attached as an APK (Android 12 or later).
