## Vintage players

Now Playing can become a real-looking player, on the Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android: choose the look with the round button at the top of Now Playing (on Android, Look in Now Playing's menu or Settings, Now Playing Look). Each is made of photographed walnut, brushed aluminum, felt and black plastic, and every control works.

- **Turntable:** the record turns with the album cover as its label, and the tonearm moves in as the song plays; drag the tonearm to go to another part of the song. Aluminum keys, the song on Dymo tape, a red LED clock, toggle switches for shuffle and repeat, and a volume knob.
- **Cassette deck:** the cassette behind a smoked door with its reels winding, VU meters following the music's left and right levels, a rolling tape counter, and piano keys (hold Rewind or Fast Forward to scan).
- **CD player:** a top-loading well with the disc printed with the cover, and a fluorescent display with the time, the song stepping through and a position bar to drag.
- **Every screen:** phones, foldables folded or open (a Galaxy Z Fold, an iPhone Duo), the two halves of a half-open fold, iPads and Mac windows. The lyrics and Up Next show behind smoked glass in the player's place.

## Your own cassettes

- Fourteen designs come with the app, nine of them after the classic tapes of the 1980s and 1990s.
- Make your own: colors, label, bands, the song written by hand, or your own pictures of the shell and the label drawn on the template (Save Template).
- Share one as a `.cassette` file: it opens on the Mac, iPhone and iPad, and Android imports it (shared from Android as a zip).

## Groovelight for Android

- **Swipe to change songs:** a swipe let go halfway no longer leaves the cover off to one side; it springs back.
- **Now Playing:** "Playing From" is centered on the screen.
- **Mini player:** swiping it down to close no longer starts opening Now Playing first.

## Installing

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Groovelight to Applications (macOS 15 or later). Earlier versions update themselves to this one.

Groovelight for Android (`Groovelight-0.7.0.apk`, Android 12 or later) installs over earlier versions, keeping its pairing and music, and updates itself from them. The Claude Desktop extension (`Groovelight-0.7.0.mcpb`) is attached below as well.
